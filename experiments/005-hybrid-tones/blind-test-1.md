# Blind recognition test 1 — hybrid tone-field (run-quarter, 20x25)

Evaluator: fresh agent, shown only the three solution PNGs. Intended: A cat, B sailboat, C tree.

- **A**: "a blocky navy building with windows on a cream sky above an olive ground." Structure 3/5, ground 4/5, sky 4/5. Cat not named. Grid 4/5 (stepped edges, "lattice of white holes" = the transition lobes).
- **B**: "a navy T-shaped column on a horizontal slab over an olive ground." Boat not named (sail invisible: light on light). Grid 4/5.
- **C**: "a tree with a round navy canopy and a long trunk on a green lawn under a cream sky." Canopy 4/5, trunk 4/5. Recognized. Grid 3/5.
- **Comparison**: "variations of one composition, not the same picture"; the figure is the real difference. Distinctiveness 4/5. Squinting helps all three.

## Conclusions

1. Distinctiveness holds (4/5, as in experiment 004) and the tree is reliably recognized; the cat and boat silhouettes need redesign (bigger head/ears; mid-tone sail), which is in progress.
2. The transition lobes read as a "regular lattice of white holes" along silhouettes and reveal the grid rhythm; rougher, edge-spanning masks are in progress.
3. Frame cells are plain (engine limitation), producing the square olive/navy blocks at the picture edges that evaluators flag as anomalies.
