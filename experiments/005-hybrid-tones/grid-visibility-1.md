# Grid visibility review 1 — hybrid tone-field (run-quarter)

- About 90% of interior grid lines are invisible in C (tree); leaks are the straight ground top (type a), the trunk verticals and a clipped olive block at the frame (type c). No same-texture seams.
- The dark/light boundary is "a row of rounded bumps with white holes" repeating at cell pitch, so the grid shows faintly through the rhythm although the boundary is not straight.
- A (cat) leaks more: rectilinear body sides, hard frame blocks. Verdicts: C 2/5, A 3/5 (best so far; previous best was 3/5).
- Scattered surplus transition blobs in the background read as deliberate stipple, not mistakes.
- Recommended: jitter large boundaries off the cell lattice (the ground line), span transition masses across the full piece width with ragged outlines, and give frame cells transitions too.
