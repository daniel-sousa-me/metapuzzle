## Conclusion

Faint chips help the metrics (scene-level overlap collapsed) but not a human solver. Uniform fields are the inherent weak spot of a tone-field grammar: with connector classes that cannot be made distinct, a sky of near-identical pieces offers neither visual nor mechanical feedback. The remedy within the rules is to make background pieces individually legible with small self-contained motifs (stars, flowers, leaves, ripples, pebbles, cloud chips), assigned by region per picture so that sorting pieces by motif narrows each one to a region, with the same motif reading differently in each picture. This is what the owner's reference images do (flowers in the meadow, chips in the sky).
