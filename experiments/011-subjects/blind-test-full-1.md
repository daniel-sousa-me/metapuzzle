# Blind recognition test 1 — "Field, Wood and Water", 27x38 full render (run-full)

Evaluator: fresh agent, shown only the three solution PNGs. Intended: A hare at dawn, B owl at dusk, C swan at midnight.

- **A (hare)**: "an olive two-pronged shape on a cream field with navy masses… like an abstract map or a seated creature." Creature 2/5. Not recognized. Grid 3/5.
- **B (owl)**: "a dark navy owl- or cat-like face with pointed ears… at dusk or night." Owl/cat head 4/5, eyes 4/5. Recognized, with the dusk mood. Grid 3/5 (rectangular eyes and nose).
- **C (swan)**: "a cream, bird-like shape (possibly a swan with a raised neck) on navy water." Swan 2/5. Partly recognized. Grid 3/5.
- **Comparison**: "variations of one style, not the same picture"; subjects and layouts differ. Distinctiveness 4/5. Squinting helps all three.
- Artefacts: stair-stepped boundaries (2-cell steps from resampled pixel art), hard-edged navy chip blocks (the S tier without transitions), rectangular eyes/nose, rectangular notches at the frame.

## Conclusions

1. The value-inversion set works: three different subjects and moods at 4/5 distinctiveness, the owl recognized blind with its mood.
2. The hare must be redrawn (in progress, 27x38-v2); the swan needs a clearer neck/head and the water should read as water (wave tier is present but weak).
3. Fixes in progress: L–S transition classes for the chip tier; drawing at 27x38 instead of resampling 20x25 to remove 2-cell steps; round eyes/moon via dot/motif classes instead of rectangles.
